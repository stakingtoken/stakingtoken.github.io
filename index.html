How to Use StakingToken

01 — ConnectConnect an EVM wallet. If the wallet is on another chain, switch to RicheChain.
02 — ApproveApprove the StakingToken contract to spend the BTG amount you intend to stake.
03 — StakeEnter BTG and optionally an upline on your first stake. Keep RIC for gas.
Why do I need RIC?
RIC is RicheChain's native gas currency. BTG is the ERC-20 asset being staked. RIC is not your staking amount.
Why do I need to approve BTG?
BTG is an ERC-20 token. Approval permits the StakingToken contract to transfer the selected amount when you stake.
Why is the wallet button red?
Red means the wallet is disconnected or on the wrong chain. Green means the wallet is connected to RicheChain. Click the button when a switch is required.
What is the 200% max cap?
The dashboard reads the cap and claimed amount from the StakingToken contract. On-chain rules determine the actual claimable amount.
How does the upline work?
The upline is recorded by the contract when its conditions allow registration. Use an address you trust and verify it before signing.
Can the live ROI number change?
Yes. The displayed live value is an estimate between blockchain reads. The smart contract is always the source of truth for a transaction.
What if a transaction fails?
Check RicheChain, RIC gas balance, BTG balance and allowance. A reverted transaction does not change the blockchain state.
Does the DApp need my seed phrase?
No. Never enter a seed phrase or private key into a website. Transactions are signed by your wallet.
Always verify the StakingToken contract address and transaction details in your wallet before signing.
