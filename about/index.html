What is Bintang (BTG)?

Bintang, ticker BTG, is a digital token deployed on RicheChain. BTG is designed as the core asset for the Staking Token ecosystem, with utility centered on staking participation and access to the BTG market.

The maximum supply is 1,000,000,000 BTG with 18 decimals. The supply plan intentionally separates tokens for staking rewards and initial market access from a larger long-term reserve.

Core principle: the project aims to keep the amount of BTG entering general circulation controlled while retaining resources for future ecosystem growth.

Controlled circulating supply

The project aims to maintain circulating supply around 11–12% of the maximum supply. For 1 billion BTG, that corresponds to approximately 110 million–120 million BTG.

The remaining supply is not intended for immediate general circulation. Keeping a reserve gives the ecosystem flexibility to fund future requirements without assuming that the full maximum supply should be released at launch.

Why target a controlled range?
  • Limit the amount of supply entering the open market at launch.
  • Maintain a dedicated staking reward allocation.
  • Keep resources available for future ecosystem development.
  • Provide flexibility for future liquidity and market-access initiatives.

Circulating-supply figures may be calculated differently by different market-data platforms. The 11–12% figure is a project target range, not a guarantee that every tracker will display the same figure at all times.

BTG staking utility

A primary utility of BTG is participation in the StakingToken staking system on RicheChain. Users can stake BTG through the DApp and interact directly with the deployed staking contract using a compatible wallet.

Staking is different from simply holding BTG. A staking transaction interacts with a smart contract and requires RIC for RicheChain gas.

Long-term reserve

The majority of BTG remains in the deployer-controlled reserve for planned and future ecosystem requirements. The reserve is intended to support the project's development over a longer time horizon rather than putting the entire supply into circulation immediately.

Potential future uses may include:

  • Expansion to additional blockchain networks.
  • Future DEX liquidity requirements.
  • Potential CEX listing and market-access programs.
  • Strategic partnerships and integrations.
  • Product, infrastructure and security development.
  • Marketing and community-growth programs.
  • Future ecosystem incentive programs.
Important: reserve tokens can affect circulating supply if they are transferred or deployed in the future. Users should monitor on-chain activity and official disclosures rather than assuming that reserve tokens will never move.

Risks to understand

BTG is a digital asset. Its supply design, staking utility and ecosystem plans do not guarantee a market price, profit or liquidity.

Market and liquidity risk

BTG's market price can fluctuate and available liquidity may change. Larger trades may experience slippage.

Smart-contract risk

Blockchain software can contain vulnerabilities, implementation errors or unexpected behavior. Users interact with smart contracts at their own risk.

Network risk

RicheChain, wallets, RPC services and DEX interfaces can experience outages, congestion or technical failures.

Supply and reserve risk

Future use of reserve tokens can change circulating supply. Users should follow actual on-chain movements and project disclosures.
