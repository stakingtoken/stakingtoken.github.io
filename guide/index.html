What is StakingToken?

StakingToken is an on-chain BTG staking application built for RicheChain. Instead of asking a user to send BTG to a person or centralized account, the staking action is executed by the smart contract. The contract records the user's stake, calculates pending ROI from elapsed time, and processes eligible reward and network-bonus distributions according to its rules.

The important distinction is that staking rewards are contract rules, not a promise made by a website interface. Your wallet signs blockchain transactions, and the smart contract determines whether a transaction succeeds or reverts.

How BTG staking works

1

Connect your wallet

Open the StakingToken DApp with a Web3-compatible wallet. The wallet should be connected to RicheChain. If the wallet is on another network, switch to RicheChain before sending transactions.

2

Choose the stake amount

Enter the amount of BTG you want to stake. The contract enforces its configured minimum stake. The DApp should show the current minimum before you submit.

3

Approve BTG

Because BTG is an ERC-20 style token, the staking contract needs an allowance to transfer the selected amount from your wallet. The approval transaction is separate from the stake transaction.

4

Stake BTG

After approval, submit the stake transaction. The contract transfers the BTG into the staking contract and records your staking position. The first stake can also register the referral/upline address supplied by the DApp.

5

Accumulate pending ROI

Pending ROI is calculated from your staked amount, the configured daily ROI rate, and elapsed time since the last checkpoint. The calculation is proportional to time; it does not require the interface to remain open.

6

Claim when available

When you claim ROI, the contract records the reward, resets the reward checkpoint, distributes eligible network bonuses, and transfers the ROI to your wallet.

How the 1% daily ROI is calculated

The contract's default dailyRoiBps is 100 BPS, which equals 1%. The calculation is based on the total amount currently recorded as staked.

daily ROI = staked BTG × dailyRoiBps ÷ 10,000
new reward = daily ROI × elapsed seconds ÷ 86,400

For example, if the configured rate is 1% and you stake 10,000 BTG, the nominal daily ROI is 100 BTG. After roughly half a day, the time-based calculation is roughly 50 BTG, subject to integer arithmetic and the contract's cap.

Important: this is not automatically compounded. The contract calculates ROI from the recorded stake amount. Claiming a reward does not automatically add that reward to your principal stake.

Can the daily ROI change?

Yes. The contract stores the daily ROI as an on-chain parameter. The deployed source defines the initial value as 100 BPS (1%), but it is a configurable parameter. Therefore, the rate shown by the live DApp and the current verified contract state should be treated as the current rate.

Understanding the 200% maximum cap

Each staking position has a maximum cap equal to 200% of the recorded stake amount. If you stake 10,000 BTG, the initial cap is 20,000 BTG.

The cap is enforced by the contract when calculating pending ROI. Once the recorded claimed amount reaches the user's cap, pending ROI becomes zero until the position is changed through another valid staking action.

Do not interpret “200% cap” as a guaranteed return. The cap is a maximum accounting limit in the contract. It does not guarantee that a user will receive a particular amount, and it does not remove smart-contract, token-liquidity, network, or market risks.

What happens if you add more BTG?

The contract allows a user with an existing stake to add more BTG. Before increasing the stake, the contract checkpoints the currently pending ROI into saved reward, then increases the recorded total amount and recalculates the 200% cap from the new total.

Because this changes the staking principal and cap, users should treat a top-up as a new on-chain state change rather than assuming it behaves exactly like a separate independent position.

How claiming works

Claim ROI

Claim ROI requests the currently available staking reward. The contract requires a positive pending reward. After a successful claim, the reward checkpoint is reset and the claimed amount is increased.

Claim sponsor bonus

Referral matching rewards are accumulated separately in pendingSponsorBonus. They are not automatically mixed into your ROI claim. If you have a positive sponsor balance, use Claim Sponsor Bonus to transfer that accumulated bonus to your wallet.

Two reward balances can therefore exist: pending staking ROI and pending sponsor/referral bonus. Check both before assuming your total available reward is zero.

BTG referral and 15-level matching system

StakingToken supports a sponsor/upline structure with up to 15 levels. The referral relationship is registered when a user makes their first qualifying stake with an eligible upline address. The contract then walks upward through the upline chain when ROI is claimed.

Referral rewards are based on claimed ROI

This is a critical detail: the matching calculation uses the ROI amount actually claimed by the downline as its base. It is not a percentage of the downline's original stake.

level bonus = claimed ROI × level BPS ÷ 10,000

For example, if a downline claims 100 BTG ROI and Level 1 is configured at 5%, the theoretical Level 1 matching amount is 5 BTG.

Referral qualification

An upline must have an active recorded stake and must not have reached their own maximum cap for the matching amount to be credited. If a level cannot receive its matching bonus, the contract does not simply ignore the economic amount: undistributed matching amounts are sent to the contract's designated leftover wallet according to the contract rules.

Referral percentages

The contract contains 15 configurable sponsor levels. The deployed source initializes them at 0%, so the actual percentages must be checked on-chain or in the live DApp rather than assumed from a static page.

Level How to interpret the rate
L1 Percentage of a qualifying direct/downline's claimed ROI
L2 Percentage of the qualifying claimed ROI from the second upline level
L3–L15 Same principle, continuing upward through the registered upline chain

Referral income is therefore performance-dependent: there must be qualifying downline activity and an actual ROI claim before a matching amount can be generated.

Leader bonus

The contract also supports a separate leader mechanism. The default leader bonus parameter in the source is 5% (500 BPS). When a user claims ROI, the contract searches upward through the upline chain for the first wallet marked as a leader.

If an eligible leader is found, the leader bonus is calculated from the same claimed ROI amount:

leader bonus = claimed ROI × leader BPS ÷ 10,000

Unlike matching bonuses, the source specifies that this leader payment is processed outside the user's 200% max-cap accounting.

Important: the 5% figure is the contract's default parameter, not a permanent guarantee. Always verify the current on-chain setting before relying on a displayed rate.

Why RIC is required

BTG is the staking asset, while RIC is the native gas currency of RicheChain. You do not stake RIC through this DApp. You keep RIC in your wallet so the wallet can pay network transaction fees for approvals, staking, claims, and other on-chain actions.

What makes BTG staking interesting?

  • On-chain accounting: staking state and reward calculations are handled by a smart contract rather than a private spreadsheet.
  • Time-based ROI: pending ROI accrues from elapsed time under the configured rate.
  • Defined maximum cap: the contract uses a 200% cap for the staking position's claim accounting.
  • Separate sponsor rewards: qualifying network bonuses are tracked separately from the main ROI balance.
  • 15-level structure: the contract supports a multi-level sponsor chain rather than a single referral tier.
  • Leader layer: the contract has a separate leader bonus mechanism.
  • Non-custodial wallet signing: users initiate and sign their own transactions.
  • RicheChain-native execution: transactions and contract state are executed on RicheChain.

These features describe the mechanics of the system; they should not be interpreted as a promise that BTG will appreciate in price or that staking will produce a guaranteed economic profit.

Risks every user should understand

Smart-contract risk

Smart contracts can contain vulnerabilities, unexpected behavior, or configuration risks. A user should never stake more than they can afford to lose.

Token and market risk

BTG's market value can move independently of the staking reward amount. Receiving more BTG does not automatically mean receiving more value in a fiat currency.

Liquidity risk

A reward being recorded on-chain does not guarantee that BTG can be sold for a desired price. Market liquidity and slippage can change over time.

Network risk

Transactions depend on RicheChain availability, RPC connectivity, wallet compatibility, and sufficient RIC for gas.

Parameter risk

The contract contains configurable parameters. A user should rely on the current verified contract state rather than assuming that a default parameter will remain unchanged forever.

Referral risk

Referral rewards depend on actual qualifying activity and the configured sponsor levels. A referral link is not a guaranteed source of income.

Troubleshooting

“Wrong network” or wallet will not connect

Switch the wallet to RicheChain, then reconnect. Confirm Chain ID 132026.

Approve works but Stake fails

Check that the approved BTG amount covers the intended stake, that your BTG balance is sufficient, that the amount meets the current minimum stake, and that you have enough RIC for gas.

Pending reward shows zero

Check whether you have an active stake, whether the transaction is confirmed, whether the maximum cap has been reached, and whether the current ROI parameter is non-zero.

Claim says no ROI available

The contract requires a positive pending reward. Wait for additional elapsed time or check whether the cap has already been reached.

Referral bonus is zero

Possible reasons include no qualifying downline ROI claim, a zero sponsor percentage at the relevant level, an upline without an active stake, or an upline who has reached their maximum cap.

Why did the referral not change?

The upline is designed to be registered on the first qualifying stake. Verify the referral before making that first transaction.

Frequently asked questions

What token do I stake?

You stake BTG, using the BTG token contract configured for this StakingToken deployment.

What network does StakingToken use?

RicheChain, Chain ID 132026.

What token pays gas?

RIC, the native currency of RicheChain.

Is the default ROI 1% per day?

Yes. The source contract initializes dailyRoiBps to 100 BPS, equal to 1%. The parameter is configurable, so verify the live on-chain value.

Is the 1% automatically compounded?

No. The contract calculates ROI from the recorded stake amount. Claimed rewards are not automatically added to the principal.

What is the maximum cap?

The contract sets maxCap to twice the recorded stake amount, equivalent to 200% of the stake amount.

When does ROI start?

The contract uses elapsed time from the recorded last-claim/checkpoint timestamp. The calculation is proportional to elapsed time.

Do I need to keep the website open?

No. The blockchain timestamp and contract state determine elapsed time. The interface is only used to read the state and submit transactions.

Do I have to claim every day?

No daily website session is required. Claim timing is a user choice subject to the contract's available reward and cap rules.

What is the referral system?

The contract supports a 15-level upline structure. Matching bonuses are calculated from qualifying downline ROI claims, not directly from the downline's stake principal.

Does a referral bonus appear automatically in my wallet?

No. Eligible matching rewards accumulate as pending sponsor bonus and can be claimed through the sponsor-bonus claim function.

What is the leader bonus?

The contract has a separate leader mechanism. Its default parameter is 5% of a qualifying claimed ROI, but the current on-chain parameter should be checked.

Can the owner change staking parameters?

The contract contains owner-controlled configuration functions. Users should therefore verify current contract state and not assume every default parameter is permanently fixed.

Can I stake more later?

Yes, the contract supports topping up an existing stake. The contract checkpoints pending ROI and updates the recorded total and cap when the additional stake is processed.

Can I withdraw my original BTG whenever I want?

The provided contract does not expose a normal user function that withdraws the original staked principal independently. Users should understand the actual contract functions before staking.

Is staking profitable?

No outcome is guaranteed. The contract defines reward calculations, but token price, liquidity, smart-contract, network, and configuration risks remain.

What happens if BTG price falls?

Your reward is denominated in BTG. A larger BTG balance can still be worth less in another currency if the market price declines.

Why do I need RIC if I stake BTG?

RIC is required to pay RicheChain network gas. BTG is the staking asset.

What should I do if a transaction fails?

Read the wallet error, check network, balance, allowance, minimum stake, cap status, and RIC gas balance. Do not repeatedly sign transactions you do not understand.

Does StakingToken need my private key?

No. Transactions are signed by your connected wallet. Never disclose a seed phrase or private key.
