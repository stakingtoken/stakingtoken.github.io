7. Staking mechanics

When a qualifying stake transaction succeeds, the contract records the user's staking amount. The deployed contract supports an initial minimum stake requirement and uses the configured staking token address.

The contract's source initializes the daily ROI parameter at 100 BPS, equivalent to 1% per day. This is a contract parameter and should not be treated as a permanent or guaranteed rate.

The contract calculates pending ROI from the recorded staking amount, the configured ROI rate and elapsed time. The calculation is time-based rather than dependent on keeping the website open.

No automatic compounding: claimed ROI is not automatically added to the recorded principal. A user should not assume that a 1% daily rate means the principal automatically increases by compounding.

8. Maximum reward cap

The contract uses a maximum cap equal to twice the recorded staking amount, commonly described as a 200% cap.

For example, a recorded stake of 10,000 BTG produces a maximum accounting cap of 20,000 BTG for the relevant staking position under the contract's cap logic.

The cap is a smart-contract accounting limit. It is not a promise that the user will necessarily receive the maximum amount, nor is it a guarantee of economic profit or market value.

9. Claiming staking ROI

A user may request the pending ROI through the DApp when the contract determines that a positive amount is available.

The contract checkpoints reward time and increases the recorded claimed amount when a claim succeeds. The contract also processes applicable sponsor and leader distributions according to its configured rules.

Claiming requires a blockchain transaction and therefore requires RIC for network gas.

10. Referral and sponsor system

The staking contract supports a sponsor/upline structure of up to 15 levels. The referral relationship is associated with a user's first qualifying stake and is subject to the contract's registration rules.

Sponsor matching is calculated from qualifying ROI actually claimed by a downline. It is not simply a percentage of the downline's initial staking principal.

The contract contains 15 configurable sponsor levels. The deployed source initializes these levels to zero until they are configured by the contract's authorized owner function. Therefore, referral percentages are not represented by these Terms as a permanent fixed schedule.

For a qualifying level, the general calculation is:

matching bonus = claimed ROI × configured level BPS ÷ 10,000

An upline may need an active stake and available cap capacity to qualify for a sponsor distribution. Amounts that do not qualify for a particular upline are handled according to the contract's distribution rules.

11. Leader bonus

The contract contains a separate leader mechanism. Its source initializes the leader bonus parameter at 500 BPS, equivalent to 5% of a qualifying claimed ROI.

The contract searches through the upline structure for an address marked as a leader and, where the contract's conditions are satisfied, processes the leader bonus from the claimed ROI amount.

The 5% value is a configurable contract parameter and is therefore not a permanent guarantee. Users should verify the current on-chain configuration.
